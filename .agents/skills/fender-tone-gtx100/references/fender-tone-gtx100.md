# Fender Tone / Mustang GTX100 Reference

## Official Support Facts

- Fender Tone is a free iPhone, Android, and Desktop app for controlling/managing compatible Fender connected amplifiers.
- Mustang GTX100 is supported by Fender Tone on iOS and Android via Bluetooth.
- Fender Tone Desktop supports LT-series/Rumble LT over USB; it does not support GTX, GT, or Rumble Stage/Studio amps.
- Initial mobile setup walks through account sign-in, Bluetooth connection to the amplifier, Wi-Fi setup for the amp, and product registration.
- Android 6.0+ requires location permissions enabled when using Bluetooth discovery.
- In the preset list under My Presets, selecting a preset opens preset detail; while the preset detail is active, the amp should update in real time.
- In preset detail, swipe up/down to cycle presets; swipe left from preset detail to enter the signal chain.
- To add an effect, navigate to preset detail or a chain item, tap the lower-left `+`, choose Pre-FX or Post-FX, pick an effect, and save.
- Parameter edits are made by selecting a preset, tapping the chain item, tapping a knob, then scrolling vertically on the full-screen knob control. Vertical scrubbing directly on a knob can make faster/finer adjustments.

## GTX100 Product/Manual Notes

- Mustang GTX100 has 200 onboard presets.
- The GTX line supports modular signal path editing and moving effects in the chain.
- The Fender Tone app can create/modify presets, back up settings, and download/share presets with the online community over Bluetooth.
- When Fender Tone is connected to Mustang GTX, the amp display shows a `TONE` icon.
- Bluetooth audio volume can be controlled from the external device or the amp's Bluetooth menu.

## Practical Troubleshooting

- If Fender Tone cannot see the amp, verify the amp is nearby, powered on, and Bluetooth is enabled on the amp.
- If Android cannot discover the amp, verify app location permission and system location are enabled.
- If another device is connected to the amp, the amp may not appear as available to this phone.
- If control is slow or inconsistent, avoid having separate Bluetooth audio and Tone control sessions fighting across devices; use the dedicated phone as the single owner while automating.
